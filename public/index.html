Weekly schedule

Weekly workout plan

Import and export

Your tasks in formats other software reads. Separate from the backup below — importing here adds tasks, it never replaces what you have.

CSV (comma, semicolon or tab separated), Markdown checklists, JSON, .ics calendar files, or a plain list with one task per line. Columns are matched by name, so exports from most task apps work unchanged. A file it cannot read is refused rather than guessed at.

An .ics file's to-dos are imported when it has any — that is what Apple Reminders exports. A calendar with only events becomes one task per event.

Housekeeping

Completed tasks are deleted once they reach this age. The generated workout rows are one a day forever, and your workout log already records what you actually did.

Everything lives in one database. A copy you hold yourself is the only thing that survives losing access to it.

Automatic backup to Google Drive, every Sunday morning. The app can only see the folder it creates - it has no access to the rest of your Drive.

Restoring replaces everything currently in the app. A copy of the current state is saved to Drive first, so a restore can be undone. Signed-in devices and notifications are left alone.

Signed-in devices

Each device holds its own session, not your passphrase. Sign one out here and it stops working immediately, without affecting the others.
