To Do

A private task list for one person, running on Cloudflare Workers.

It keeps a single ranked list of what to do next, weighing deadlines against priority and how long each thing takes. Every morning at six it sends one push notification suggesting what to start with, and it reads a clinical on-call schedule to know how much of the day is already spoken for.

What it does

  • Tasks with deadlines, estimates, subtasks, and recurrence
  • A daily push notification with the day's suggested focus
  • Weekly commitments and an on-call schedule feed, so the workload total is honest
  • Weekly backups to the owner's own Google Drive

Who it is for

One person - the author. It is not a product, it has no sign-up, and there is nothing here to join. It is published only so that its author's own Google account can authorise it to write backup files to their own Drive.
