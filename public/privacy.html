Privacy policy

Last updated 24 August 2026.

To Do is a personal task application with a single user, who is also its author. It has no sign-up, no other accounts, and no way for anyone else to store data in it. This policy describes what that one person's data does.

What is stored

  • Tasks, notes, deadlines, estimates, and completion history
  • Recurring weekly commitments
  • A workout plan and log
  • An imported work schedule, used to estimate how much of each day is already committed
  • Web push subscriptions for the devices that receive the daily notification
  • Sign-in sessions, stored as hashes rather than as tokens

All of it lives in a Cloudflare D1 database in the United States, reachable only by this application.

What is not stored

There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party scripts of any kind. Nothing is sold, shared, or transmitted to anyone. No patient information or other identifiable third-party data is entered into the application.

Google Drive

The application can upload a weekly backup of its own database to its owner's Google Drive. It requests the drive.file scope only, which allows an application to see and manage exclusively the files it created itself. It cannot read, list, or modify any other file in the Drive account, and it never attempts to.

Backups exclude push subscriptions and sign-in sessions: those are device credentials, they are recreated simply by signing in again, and cloud storage is the wrong place for them.

Authorisation can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately and permanently ends the application's access.

Retention

Completed tasks are deleted automatically after a configurable period. The twelve most recent backups are kept and older ones are deleted. Deleting the database deletes everything.
